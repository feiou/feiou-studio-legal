HabitTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026
Effective: September 25, 2026

About this policy

HabitTab is operated by Feiou Su (Feiou Studio). This policy describes how the HabitTab app and its widgets handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@feioustudio.com.

HabitTab helps you record habits, routines, and optional medication information. You do not create a HabitTab account. Your records are stored on your device and can sync through your Apple iCloud account.

Information the app handles

Information you enter includes board and habit names, schedules, targets, completion dates and times, counts, and optional medication amounts and units. The app also stores board appearance choices, archive and pause status, and identifiers that keep records associated with the correct board.

Medication names, doses, and habits may reveal sensitive health information. Enter only information you want stored and synced as described below. HabitTab does not read from or write to Apple Health and does not send your records to an AI service.

Local preferences include language, sound and haptic settings, onboarding state, and usage counters used to decide when to ask for an App Store review. These counters are not sent to a developer analytics service.

Device motion is processed temporarily on your device to animate board tilt. HabitTab does not save or transmit the motion readings. Sound playback does not record your microphone.

Storage, iCloud, and widgets

HabitTab uses a local database shared with its own widgets so they can display boards and record completions. Widget content can be visible to someone who can see your device; you can remove widgets using the system controls.

The app is configured to sync boards and records, including any medication information you enter, through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Changes made offline can sync later. Availability depends on your Apple account, device settings, connectivity, and Apple services.

Apple processes iCloud information under its own privacy policy and service terms. We do not operate a separate server that receives your habit or medication database. Device and iCloud backups may retain additional copies according to your Apple settings.

How information is used and shared

Your records are used to display your boards, calculate progress and statistics, maintain history, operate widgets, and sync between your devices. Preferences personalize the app.

The current app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or a developer analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information or share your habit or medication records for advertising.

Apple provides app distribution, iCloud, and system services, and may process service and diagnostic information under its own terms and your device settings. HabitTab does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve the request. Avoid sending medication details or other sensitive information unless needed. Our email provider processes support correspondence. We may disclose correspondence when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and your choices

Your boards and history remain stored until removed from the relevant storage. You can edit boards, correct records, and undo completions in the app. Pausing or archiving a board retains its data; it does not delete it. The current app does not provide a delete-all control for your personal boards.

You can manage HabitTab’s iCloud access and stored cloud data using the available controls in Apple account and iCloud settings. Disabling sync alone does not delete existing cloud records. Removing the app removes its local app data but does not by itself delete iCloud records or backups, and records may return after reinstalling and syncing. Offloading an app preserves its local data.

To remove retained copies, manage app storage, iCloud storage, and backups separately on your Apple devices. Other devices may retain local copies. Contact support for guidance; we cannot directly access or erase your private iCloud records for you.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence we control, subject to those obligations.

Privacy rights and security

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, request portability, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact support@feioustudio.com to exercise rights concerning information we hold. We may ask for information needed to verify a request.

Local and iCloud records are primarily controlled through your device and Apple account. Apple and email services may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards and terms.

We rely on platform storage protections and Apple’s iCloud security features. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Protect your device and Apple account, and consider what information appears in widgets.

Children and changes

HabitTab is a general-purpose habit tracker. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support without any consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us.

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required.
