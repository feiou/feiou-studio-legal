Privacy Policy

Last updated August 5, 2026 · Effective August 5, 2026

PlanTrack is operated by Feiou Su ("PlanTrack," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the PlanTrack iOS and iPadOS app handles information.

PlanTrack is local-first. You can track one plan without signing in. Some information still leaves your device for limited purposes such as anonymous authentication, privacy-conscious analytics, purchase verification, and features you choose to enable. Cross-device content sync happens only for a Pro user who signs in with Apple.

1. Information we handle

Information you enter or import

This can include fitness or wellness goals, preferences, constraints, optional age, sex, height and weight, imported plans and revisions, exercise notes, completion history, set counts, timers, streaks, metrics, Coach conversations, AI proposals, and app settings. Some of this information may reveal health or fitness information.

Apple Health information

If you are a Pro user and choose to connect Apple Health, PlanTrack requests read-only access to the types you approve: steps, active energy, resting heart rate, body weight, sleep, and workouts. PlanTrack does not write to Apple Health.

Health information is queried on demand to show progress and prepare progress or Coach context. PlanTrack does not save Apple Health samples in its database, sync them to its cloud database, send their values to analytics or RevenueCat, use them for advertising or marketing, or sell them.

Your Health information can leave your device only when you direct an AI-related workflow:

  • Apple Intelligence: processing occurs on your device through Apple's system framework.
  • Your API key: the relevant prompt, which may include Health summaries, is sent directly to the OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configured. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Copy and paste: a progress report may be placed on the system clipboard. It reaches another service only if you paste or share it there.

You can control Health permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Account and device identifiers

On first launch, the App creates a random installation/account identifier in Keychain and attempts anonymous authentication with Supabase. Anonymous authentication does not upload your plans or progress. If you choose Sign in with Apple for sync, we receive the Supabase user ID and authentication information needed to maintain the session. Apple may provide an email or private relay address according to your settings. We do not request your name.

Purchase information

When Pro purchases are available, Apple processes payment. RevenueCat receives a random App User ID, receipt and transaction information, product and entitlement status, and related metadata needed to offer, verify, restore, and support the purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics and diagnostics

When analytics is configured, TelemetryDeck receives feature events, counts and booleans, permission outcomes, request result categories, purchase-flow events, sync status, a hashed installation identifier, an hour-level timestamp, and basic app, OS, device, language, and locale metadata. Events are designed not to include plan or prompt text, Coach messages, Health or manually entered metric values, API keys, Apple email addresses, or the PlanTrack account identifier. TelemetryDeck states that it does not store IP addresses for App SDK events.

Support communications

If you contact us, we process the email address, account ID, screenshots, and message you choose to provide so we can respond and troubleshoot. Do not send medical records, API keys, or unrelated sensitive information.

2. Where information is stored

On your device

Plans, revisions, progress, manually entered metrics, and Coach conversations are stored locally. Preferences and short-lived workflow state use local app or App Group storage. Partial set counts are pruned after approximately 14 days. The account ID, authentication session, and any AI API key are stored in Keychain. The API key is not included in analytics, PlanTrack sync, or purchase data and is sent only to the endpoint you configure.

Notifications are scheduled locally by iOS. PlanTrack does not operate a remote push server.

Optional cloud sync

Content sync activates only if you have Pro and sign in with Apple. Synced data can include:

  • plans, source markdown, notes, status, and revision history;
  • completion records and manually entered item or daily metrics;
  • Coach conversation titles, messages, and plan proposals; and
  • identifiers and timestamps needed for synchronization and deletion.

Apple Health samples and summaries, prompt-builder profile settings, notification preferences, partial set counts, and AI API keys are not synced by PlanTrack.

3. How we use information

  • Provide plan tracking, timers, progress, plan history, and Coach features.
  • Generate, reformat, or update plans using the AI method you select.
  • Provide optional sync and restore synced content.
  • Verify purchases and unlock Pro features.
  • Schedule the local reminders you enable.
  • Understand aggregate reliability and improve the App.
  • Protect, troubleshoot, and support the Service and comply with law.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information to perform our contract with you, with consent for optional features, for legitimate operational interests that do not override your rights, and to meet legal obligations.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local content: stays until you delete it, use Settings → Reset local data, or remove the App. Reset deletes local plans, completions, Coach content, and recorded metrics, but not cloud copies, purchase records, Keychain account data, or a separately stored API key.
  • Keychain data: may survive uninstalling. Clear your AI connection before uninstalling if you want to remove its key.
  • Synced content: remains until content deletion or account deletion. Limited tombstones, logs, and backups may remain for deletion propagation, security, legal compliance, and routine backup expiration.
  • Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat keep transaction records for restoration, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal requirements.
  • Analytics and support: are retained according to provider configuration and operational, legal, and security needs. De-identified aggregate data may be kept longer.

To request deletion of your PlanTrack cloud account and content, email support@feioustudio.com with subject PlanTrack account deletion and the account ID shown in Settings. We may verify control of the account. We complete verified requests without undue delay and within the period required by applicable law, unless law permits or requires longer. Account deletion does not cancel, refund, or erase an App Store purchase record.

6. Your choices and rights

You can use the core tracker without Apple sign-in; revoke Health and notification access; turn off Apple Intelligence; remove your API key; delete content; reset local data; and request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection where applicable. Send requests to support@feioustudio.com and include your account ID for cloud or purchase-linked data. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Security and international processing

We use safeguards including Keychain storage, encrypted network connections, access controls, and Supabase row-level authorization. No method is completely secure. Providers may process information in other countries using recognized safeguards where required.

8. Children

The App Store listing displays PlanTrack's current content age rating. That rating describes content suitability; it does not determine whether a person can enter a contract or consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect a child's personal information without the permission or other lawful basis required where the child lives. Contact us if you believe a child provided information without appropriate permission.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, providers, or law changes. We will update the date above and provide additional notice for a material change. We will request consent before a new use when required.
