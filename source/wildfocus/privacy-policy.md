# WildFocus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Effective: November 23, 2026

## About this policy

WildFocus is operated by Feiou Su (Feiou Studio). This policy describes how the WildFocus app, its Lock Screen card, and its Screen Time extensions handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@feioustudio.com.

WildFocus is a focus timer: you choose a destination and a route length, focus while the walk runs, and keep a logbook of your focus time and the wildlife photographs each walk earns. You do not create a WildFocus account. Your logbook is stored on your device, and with WildFocus Pro it can sync through your Apple iCloud account.

## Information the app handles

Your logbook holds each walk's date, start and end times, focus and break time, destination and route, any activity label and note you add, the wildlife photographs the walk earned with their attribution, and any personal photo you choose to attach.

When you attach a personal photo, you pick it with the system photo picker. WildFocus receives only the photo you pick, not access to your photo library, and saves a copy of it in the app.

Local preferences include language, sound, haptic and display settings, your selected destination and route, onboarding progress, and whether you have unlocked Pro. WildFocus also keeps a temporary copy of a walk in progress so it can be recovered if the app is closed. None of this is sent to a developer analytics service.

WildFocus does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or Apple Health data.

## Screen Time and App Lock

App Lock is optional. If you turn it on, iOS asks for your permission through Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework, and you pick the apps and websites to hold back during a focus walk. iOS gives WildFocus only opaque tokens for your choices; WildFocus cannot see which apps you chose, how you use them, or your Screen Time history. The tokens are stored on your device and used by the app and its Screen Time extensions only to block and release those apps. They are not synced or sent to us.

## Destinations, weather, maps and wildlife photographs

The destinations are fixed, well-known places. To show their weather, map and wildlife, WildFocus contacts these services with the destination, never your own location:

- Apple WeatherKit and the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) receive the destination's coordinates to return its current weather. Requests to MET Norway identify the app and its support address, as that service requires.
- Apple MapKit provides the destination maps and globe.
- The iNaturalist API receives the destination's place and the species being looked for, and returns openly licensed (CC0 or CC BY) research-grade wildlife photographs, which are then downloaded from iNaturalist's image hosting. WildFocus shows each photographer's attribution.

Like any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request information, and handle it under their own policies. WildFocus sends them no logbook entries, notes, photos or identifiers about you. Bundled photographs are used when a service is unavailable.

## iCloud sync (WildFocus Pro)

With Pro, WildFocus syncs your logbook — walk records, notes, the wildlife photographs you earned and any personal photos you attached — through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple account have it. Without Pro, or when iCloud is unavailable, the logbook stays on the device.

Apple processes iCloud information under its own privacy policy and service terms. We do not operate a server that receives your logbook, and we cannot read your private iCloud data. Device and iCloud backups may keep additional copies according to your Apple settings.

## Lock Screen card and notifications

During a walk, WildFocus can show its destination, route and progress in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This comes from the app on your device and can be seen by anyone who can see your screen. You can turn Live Activities off in Settings.

If you allow notifications, WildFocus schedules local reminders on your device, for example when a break ends. They are not sent through our servers.

## Purchases

WildFocus Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; we do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with the App Store to confirm whether Pro is unlocked and to restore a previous purchase.

## How information is used and shared

Your information is used to run focus walks, block and release apps you chose, show your logbook and statistics, recover an interrupted walk, operate the Lock Screen card, and sync between your devices if you have Pro.

WildFocus does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or a developer analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Apple provides app distribution, purchases, iCloud and system services, and may process service and diagnostic information under its own terms and your device settings.

If you contact support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Our email provider processes support correspondence. We may disclose correspondence when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

## Retention and your choices

Your logbook stays on your device until you remove the app. The free version shows the last 7 days of walks; older walks are kept on the device, not deleted, and appear again if you unlock Pro. The current app does not provide a control for deleting individual walks.

You can turn off App Lock, notifications and Live Activities at any time, and revoke WildFocus's Screen Time permission in Settings. You can manage WildFocus's iCloud data in your Apple account's iCloud settings. Removing the app removes its local data but does not by itself delete iCloud records or backups, and synced records may return after reinstalling. Other devices may keep their own copies. Contact support for guidance; we cannot access or erase your private iCloud records for you.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records and meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence we control, subject to those obligations.

## Privacy rights and security

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to request portability, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact support@feioustudio.com to exercise rights concerning information we hold. We may ask for information needed to verify a request.

Your logbook is primarily controlled through your device and Apple account. Apple, the services named above and our email provider may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards and terms.

We rely on platform storage protections and Apple's iCloud security. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Protect your device and Apple account, and consider what appears on your Lock Screen.

## Children and changes

WildFocus is a general-purpose focus timer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support without any consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us.

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required.
